# Beyond the World

This repository documents my long‑term self‑study journey through the mathematical and physical foundations that underpin our modern description of the universe.

**What you'll find here:**

- Solved exercises from textbooks (Axler, Griffiths, etc.), written in Markdown/LaTeX.
- Numerical simulations (Python, Jupyter) of physical systems – classical mechanics, electrodynamics, relativistic kinematics.
- Notes and small projects as I progress toward more advanced topics (general relativity, quantum field theory, fluid dynamics).
- A transparent log of learning and growth.

**Why "Beyond the World"?**  
Because the physics explored here lies beyond everyday intuition – curved spacetime, quantum fluctuations, field theories – and because pushing past the horizon of one's own knowledge is a journey in itself.
